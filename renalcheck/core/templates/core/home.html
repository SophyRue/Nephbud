{# Left — text and stat cards #}
The connection most people miss

Your daily habits shape your kidney health

Most Nigerians who develop kidney disease had warning signs in their daily routines for years. The extra salt, the skipped water, the painkillers taken without a second thought. RenalCheck connects those habits to your risk, so you can do something about it while prevention still works.

Salt

High sodium intake raises blood pressure, which strains kidney function over time.

Water

Chronic under hydration is common and reversible. Small increases help your kidneys filter waste.

Family

A family history of hypertension, diabetes, or kidney disease changes your baseline risk. Worth knowing early.

Painkillers

Frequent use of over the counter analgesics and herbal remedies stresses the kidneys more than most people realise.

{# Right — lifestyle timeline #}
How risk changes with time
Your 20s and early 30s

Build good habits now

Your kidneys are resilient. The patterns you build today, hydration, diet, activity, set the foundation for decades to come.

Mid 30s to 40s

Small shifts, big impact

Blood pressure and weight start reflecting years of choices. This is the window where lifestyle changes matter most.

Late 40s and beyond

It is never too late

People who understood their risk earlier tend to have better outcomes. Acting now still makes a meaningful difference.

At any age

Awareness is the first step

RenalCheck gives you clear, honest information about your personal risk. What you do with it is up to you.

How it works

From your habits to your risk profile, in four steps

No lab visits. No medical jargon. Just honest, personalised clarity based on how you actually live.

1

Tell us about your life

Answer questions about your diet, hydration, blood pressure, family history, medication use, and daily habits. Takes under 3 minutes. No guesswork required.

2

We calculate your risk

Our clinically informed model scores your kidney risk instantly. Built around the specific risk factors most relevant to Nigerian adults, not a Western population.

3

See what is driving it

Get a clear Low, Moderate, or High risk rating and see exactly which habits are contributing to it, with plain language explanations of what each factor means for you personally.

4

Stay ahead of it

RenalCheck tracks your progress, sends intelligent reminders when you are overdue, and shows you how lifestyle changes actually move your score over time.
